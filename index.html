Farm state

Edit plot

Choose the flower breed, primary color, pattern, and secondary color. Only combinations recorded in inventory are shown. Click cells to place it, or switch to Erase to remove plants. Each cell shows its job tag, as on the map: 1A / 1B a breeding pair (A = first parent, B = each second parent), 2F a fertilizer-bed plant. "Auto" keeps a cell's tag, or reuses the tag of the same flower you moved. Pick a tag and click a planted cell to change its tag only.

Breeding recommendation

Change plot target

Actual targets describe goals for plants already recorded on the plot; changing one does not move those plants. Recommendation targets are checked against biome, inventory, reserved parent copies, the other targets on this plot, and available safe planting space.

Select a target, then check whether it can be planted here.

Plot targets

Manage plot targets

Actual targets describe goals attached to the plants recorded on this plot. Recommendation targets describe the proposed planting plan. Editing or removing one target leaves the other targets alone; removing an Actual target keeps its recorded plants in place.
